Common questions

How much does junk removal cost in San Antonio?

It depends on how much space your items take up in our truck and what they are. Send a few photos or call and we'll give you an upfront price before we start.

What won't you take?

We can't take hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, motor oil, asbestos, medical waste or propane tanks. If you're unsure about something, just ask.

Do I have to carry anything out?

No. We pick up from anywhere on your property: inside, upstairs, in the garage or out back.

What areas do you serve?

All of San Antonio plus surrounding cities from Boerne and New Braunfels to Seguin, Floresville, Pleasanton, Castroville and Hondo.

What happens to my junk?

We donate what's usable, recycle what we can and dispose of the rest properly.
