Hot Tub Removal questions

Do I need to drain the hot tub first?

Yes. Drain it and have an electrician disconnect the power before we arrive. We handle the cutting and hauling.

Will you tear up my yard?

We cut the tub into manageable pieces on site so it doesn't get dragged across your lawn.

What areas do you serve?

We provide hot tub removal throughout San Antonio and surrounding cities including Boerne, New Braunfels, Schertz, Seguin and more.

How is the price figured?

By how much space the items take up in our trailer, plus any heavy materials. You get an upfront price before we start.
