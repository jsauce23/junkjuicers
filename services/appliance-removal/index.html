Appliance Removal questions

Do you disconnect appliances?

Please have water and gas lines shut off and disconnected before we arrive. We handle the heavy lifting from there.

What happens to my old appliance?

Metal appliances are recycled for scrap whenever possible.

What areas do you serve?

We provide appliance removal throughout San Antonio and surrounding cities including Boerne, New Braunfels, Schertz, Seguin and more.

How is the price figured?

By how much space the items take up in our trailer, plus any heavy materials. You get an upfront price before we start.
